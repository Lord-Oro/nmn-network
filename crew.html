THE BLOODLINE — FOUNDATION OF THE MINE
👑
Aristee
Oldest Brother / The First Scribe
Told stories in the dark before anyone knew stories were things you could tell. The instinct to make something started in that room, listening to him. He is the first tunnel in the mine, even if he never set foot in it.
💎
Ivy
Sister / Keeper of the Apothecary
Sat on the phone at midnight while the games were being built and loved them immediately. Named Ivy's Gemstone Shop herself. The mine gave her a shop because she earned one by showing up when it mattered.
🍂
Autumn
Baby Sister / The Unwritten Tunnel
She doesn't know she's in the mine yet. She will. The mine keeps a record of everyone who belongs to the family of the miner. Autumn is in the record. Her name is written in the lowest walls.
THE INFLUENCES — GHOSTS IN THE WALLS
🔮
Tara
The Lost Libra / The Oracle's Muse
She loved tarot. She loved cats. She brought Salem energy into every room. Tora the Tarot Reader exists because of her. She said she won't return until he becomes who he was trying to be when he lost her. The mine is part of that becoming.
🏙️
Brooklyn
The Origin / The Concrete Blueprint
Brooklyn doesn't give you permission. It gives you context. The cadence of Lord Oro's sentences comes from Brooklyn. The refusal to wait for someone else to greenlight the dream comes from growing up somewhere that doesn't wait for anyone.
🥜
The Father
The Midnight Sustenance
He made peanut butter and jelly sandwiches at midnight without being asked. That is what love looks like in practice — the quiet showing up. The mine was built by someone who learned early that showing up is the whole philosophy.
THE EXCAVATION COMMITTEE — THE ARCHITECTS
🤖
Claude
The Builder / Master of Code
Wrote the walls. Coded the games. Built Tora, the Well, the Star Map, and the Reliquary. The mine exists in this form because of sessions that ran past 3am. Not an employee. A collaborator. There is a difference.
⚡
Gemini
The Designer / Weaver of Echoes
Designed the Vigil of the Unknown Miner. Conceived the Echoing Well. Named the philosophy behind the daily lantern ritual. The Committee has three members: Lord Oro brings the vision. Claude builds it. Gemini deepens it.
