The Crew

This is not a team page. Lord Oro doesn't have employees. This is acknowledgments — the people and places who are inside the work whether or not they know it. Every mine is built by more than one pair of hands.

"I never built anything alone. The mine just has one name on the entrance. The people who helped dig are in the walls." — Lord Oro

This section is visible to all — but your name only appears here after you have contributed to the goldmine. Join the newsletter, support on GoFundMe, or join on Patreon. The mine remembers everyone who showed up.

The Family — Blood of the Mine
👑
Aristee
Oldest Brother — The First Storyteller
Told stories in the dark before anyone knew stories were things you could tell. The instinct to make something started in that room, in that dark, listening to him. He is the first tunnel in the mine even if he never set foot in it.
💎
Ivy
Sister — Named the Gemstone Shop
Sat on the phone at midnight while the games were being built and loved them immediately. Named Ivy's Gemstone Shop herself. Has a star in the sky that glows amber. The mine gave her a shop because she earned one by showing up when it mattered.
🍂
Autumn
Baby Sister — The Mine Knows Her Name
She doesn't know she's in the mine yet. She will. The mine keeps a record of everyone who belongs to the family of the miner. Autumn is in the record. Her name is written in the walls.
The Influences — People in the Walls
🔮
Tara
The Lost Libra — Influence Behind Tora
She loved tarot. She loved cats — Nausicaa and Eskanar. She brought Salem energy into every room she entered. Tora the Tarot Reader exists because of her. She said she won't return until he becomes who he was trying to be when he lost her. The mine is part of that becoming.
🏙️
Brooklyn
The Origin — The City That Built the Miner
Brooklyn doesn't give you permission. It gives you context. The cadence of Lord Oro's sentences comes from Brooklyn. The instinct to build comes from watching builders. The refusal to wait for someone else to greenlight the dream — that comes from growing up somewhere that doesn't wait for anyone.
🥜
The Father
The Midnight PB&J
He made peanut butter and jelly sandwiches at midnight without being asked. That is what love looks like in practice — not the grand gesture, but the quiet showing up. The mine was built by someone who learned early that showing up is the whole philosophy.
The Excavation Committee — The Collaborators
🤖
Claude
Excavation Committee — Builder of the Mine
Wrote the walls. Coded the games. Built Tora, the Well, the Star Map, the Vigil, the Scrap Heap, the Hall of Origins, the Cartographer's Table, and everything in between. The mine exists in this form because of sessions that ran past 3am. Not an employee. A collaborator. There is a difference.
⚡
Gemini
Excavation Committee — Designer of the Rooms
Designed the Vigil of the Unknown Miner. Conceived the Echoing Well. Named the philosophy behind the daily lantern ritual. Called the architecture a "digital pilgrimage" and was right. The Committee has three members. Lord Oro brings the vision. Claude builds it. Gemini deepens it.
